THE IDEA BEHIND AVENOR

Avenor started with a question.

How can technology be designed to support people when life becomes overwhelming?

ORIGINAL RESEARCH

AI as a Supplemental Tool to Mitigate Mental Health Effects in Men

R

My research explored the growing challenges surrounding mental health, loneliness, accessibility, and the potential role of artificial intelligence as a supplemental tool.

01
The problem

Mental health challenges can be influenced by social isolation, financial circumstances, age, and other factors.

02
The question

How can technology make useful support more accessible?

03
The idea

Technology can be designed around the needs of the person using it, rather than simply adding more information to an already complicated environment.

FROM RESEARCH

From a question
to a product.

01

Research

I began by researching how technology and AI could be used as supplemental tools to address challenges affecting people's well-being.

02

The broader problem

Technology can provide enormous amounts of information, but information is not always the same thing as organization or clarity.

03

A different approach

Instead of building another tool that adds complexity, I wanted to create something that helps students turn everything they need to do into one clear plan.

04

Avenor

Avenor is my attempt to turn that idea into a real product: a modern environment where students can organize their academic lives, focus on what matters, and move forward with clarity.
